`common_passwords.txt`: lowercase, one per line. Built from the xato-net, NCSC 100k and Pwdb lists of
[SecLists](https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists) (MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2018 Daniel Miessler):
every entry of the three 100k lists with 5+ characters, plus every entry of the xato and Pwdb 1M lists
with 12+ characters (the ones that can pass the length rule).
